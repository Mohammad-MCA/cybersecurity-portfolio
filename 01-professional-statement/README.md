# 01 – Professional Statement

**Course:** Foundations of Cybersecurity  
**Portfolio Activity:** Create a professional statement for your cybersecurity portfolio

---

## Overview

A clear, concise professional statement helps employers quickly understand your background, motivation, and the value you bring as an entry-level cybersecurity analyst.

## Deliverable

See [`professional-statement.md`](./professional-statement.md) for a ready-to-customize template.

## Tips for Customization

- Keep it between 100–200 words.
- Highlight transferable skills (analysis, problem-solving, communication).
- Mention the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and any relevant tools.
- Align language with the job descriptions you target (SOC Analyst, Cybersecurity Analyst, etc.).
